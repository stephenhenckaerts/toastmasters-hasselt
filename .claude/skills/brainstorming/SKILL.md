---
name: brainstorming
description: |
  Socratic design refinement for an open problem — what to build or which
  approach. Use when: "brainstorm", "let's think through", "explore options",
  "decide between", "what's the best approach", "figure out what to build".
  Hands off to planning once a direction is chosen.
---

<skill_directive>
UNDERSTAND → EXPLORE → DESIGN → DOCUMENT, before any implementation.

1. Clarifying questions **one at a time**; purpose, constraints, success in members.
2. Two or three options with pros, cons, effort, and "does a non-technical
   board member survive it".
3. Design in 200–300 word sections, validate each.
4. Document in `docs/YYYY-MM-DD-<topic>.md`: Problem, Decision, Design,
   Alternatives, Notes. English.

YAGNI: this is a club site with six pages. Challenge every feature.
</skill_directive>
